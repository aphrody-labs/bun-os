# Craft studio previews

These JPEGs are screenshots of the assembled Craft browser editors, captured
on the VPS at 1280 × 800 with Chrome and its software WebGL renderer. WordCraft
and GridCraft show their built-in sample documents. They are used by the Craft
studio directory, not as evidence of hardware WebGPU qualification.

Capture the actual editor after its resources load. Do not replace these images
with WebOS package-discovery errors or loading screens. Application attribution,
licenses and third-party notices remain with their canonical source owners.
